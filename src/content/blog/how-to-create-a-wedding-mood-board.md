Creating a mood board for your wedding using Pinterest is a fantastic way to gather inspiration and visualise your dream wedding. Let’s dive into the steps:

### 1. Set Up Your Pinterest Account

If you don’t already have a [Pinterest](https://nz.pinterest.com/momentsinlightnz/) account, sign up for one. It’s free and easy to use.

### 2. Start a New Board

Create a new board specifically for your wedding mood board. Give it a descriptive name like “New Zealand Wedding Inspiration.”

### 3. Sections and Boards

Organise your pins by creating different sections within your board. For example:

- **Wedding Dresses**
- **Floral Arrangements**
- **Venue Ideas**
- **Eco-Friendly Decor**

Alternatively, you can create separate boards for each category.

### 4. Pin What You Love

Search for wedding-related keywords like “New Zealand wedding,” “beach wedding,” or “rustic wedding.” Pin images that resonate with you. Remember to use the description box to note what inspires you about each pin.

### 5. Explore Related Pins

Pinterest’s algorithm suggests similar pins based on what you’ve already saved. Explore these related images—they might lead you to hidden gems you wouldn’t find through regular search.

### 6. Collaborate and Share

Share your mood board with your wedding planner, photographer, florist, and other vendors. It helps them understand your vision and ensures everyone is on the same page.

### 7. Stay Inspired

Keep adding to your board as you discover new ideas. Pinterest is a treasure trove of inspiration, so enjoy the process!

We have a range of Pinterest [boards](https://nz.pinterest.com/momentsinlightnz/) that you might find helpful.

Remember, your mood board reflects your unique style and preferences. Happy pinning! 📌✨
