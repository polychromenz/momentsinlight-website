### Intro

Located in Kumeu, [**Abel Estate**](https://abelestate.co.nz/) combines a modern marquee with expansive gardens, a reflective pond, and mature trees that glow beautifully at golden hour. It’s one of those venues where you can have everything — ceremony, portraits, and reception — in one seamless space.

If you’re planning your wedding here, I’ll help you create a photography timeline that makes the most of the **gardens, water reflections, and evening light**, while still keeping things relaxed so you can enjoy time with your guests.

➡️ Explore my full-day wedding photography pricing starting from **$2,990**, or [get in touch](https://www.momentsinlight.co.nz/contact) to reserve your date.

## Best Photo Locations at Abel Estate

- **The Gardens** – private, green spaces for first looks or bridal party portraits.
- **The Pond** – a signature feature of Abel Estate; perfect for reflection shots at golden hour.
- **Tree-Lined Paths** – great for natural, candid portraits as you walk together.
- **The Marquee & Interiors** – ideal for reception shots, speeches, and first dance with clean, flattering light.
