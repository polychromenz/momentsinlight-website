Your wedding invitations set the tone for your special day, and finding the right supplier is crucial. Whether you’re planning an intimate gathering or a grand celebration in Auckland, here are some steps to help you discover the perfect invitation cards:

#### **1. Define Your Style and Theme**

Before you start your search, consider the overall style and theme of your wedding. Are you going for a rustic, modern, vintage, or minimalist vibe? Knowing your preferences will guide you in selecting invitation designs that align with your vision.

#### **3. Consider Quality and Turnaround Time**

When evaluating suppliers, pay attention to the following factors:

- **Quality**: Look for suppliers who use high-quality materials and printing techniques. Your wedding invitations should reflect elegance and durability.
- **Turnaround Time**: Ensure that the supplier can deliver within your desired timeframe. You don’t want to send out invitations late.

#### **4. Read Reviews and Ask for Samples**

Check online reviews and testimonials to learn about other couples’ experiences with the suppliers. Additionally, request samples of their work to assess the paper quality, print clarity, and overall design.

#### **5. Budget Wisely**

Invitations are an essential part of your wedding budget. Consider how many guests you’re inviting and allocate funds accordingly. Remember that custom designs may cost more than pre-made templates.

#### **6. Personalise Your Invitations**

Once you’ve chosen a supplier, work closely with them to personalise your invitations. Customise the wording, fonts, colours, and any additional details that make your cards uniquely yours.

Remember, your wedding invitations are the first glimpse your guests will have of your big day. Choose wisely, let your love story shine through every beautifully crafted card! 💌
