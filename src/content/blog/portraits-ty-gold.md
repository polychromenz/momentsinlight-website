I have wanted to do a shoot with gold leaf for a long time but sometimes things need to simmer, ideas need to percolate. When I found Ty I knew I was going to be able to make this work. I still think I will explore this theme more in the future, but I am really happy with these. I entered one in the NZIPP Iris awards, it got judged as high professional standard which is always a nice feeling.
