This was one of my street photoshoots. We started at one end of Ponsonby and worked our way up to the other end. Anna is new to modelling and wants to flesh out her portfolio. She came well prepared with a jacket and sunglasses so we could change the look as we went. I am really happy with the results, and I know she is thrilled with them.

It’s amazing how many styles and looks we achieved with just the various backdrops offered by different shop entrances and various side streets.
