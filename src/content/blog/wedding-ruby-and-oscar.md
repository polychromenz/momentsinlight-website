Our favourite kind of couple. Relaxed, fun and creative. And that dress is to die for!

“This was a really awesome wedding photograph. There were three main parts to the wedding. Bride and groom getting ready in their respective living arrangements, the ceremony at St Paul's Church and then the bride and groom photos with reception to follow.

They were really willing participants which was awesome, and it made the day a lot easier. The thing I really loved about this wedding was just the variety of shots I was able to create and especially with the bride and groom photos, we got some really special shots which came out well.

All up I took maybe 4,000 photos on the day. They ended up getting 520 photos edited into color and in black and white and also high and low resolution. They absolutely loved the end result and that's always the best thing for me as a photographer, the result that people receive. It really means a lot to me to know that people love the work that I do.

My favorite photo from that shoot is from the bride and groom series where there's a shot of the veil suspended in mid-air. It's absolutely stunning and I'm so proud to have created that image.

David
