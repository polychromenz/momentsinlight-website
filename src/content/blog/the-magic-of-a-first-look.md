### What is a First Look and Why It's Trending in Weddings

A first look wedding is an evolving tradition where couples choose to see each other before the ceremony. While it may seem unconventional to some, this modern wedding twist adds a layer of intimacy and excitement to the day. Instead of waiting for the big reveal at the altar, you and your partner can share a personal moment that sets the tone for the rest of the festivities. It's a chance to embrace each other, share a laugh, and perhaps shed a tear or two without the gaze of every guest upon you.

This trend is reshaping wedding planning by offering a new way to experience the day's emotions. A first look can help ease the pre-ceremony jitters, providing a calm and collected start to the celebrations. For many couples, it's about creating a memory that’s as vivid as the ceremony and reception itself. As wedding photographers capture these candid moments, they often become some of the most cherished images in a wedding album.
