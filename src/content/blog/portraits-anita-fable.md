One of my favourite things to do for portrait photography is to rent a hotel room or a historic building for a photoshoot. This makes it so easy to style the shoot as the setting is already in place. It works out much cheaper than renting props and you get a nicely styled location with everything already just so. Sometimes it’s a little cramped but it usually works out just fine. Anita is one of my regular models and she always does an awesome job. I have shot her many times and as she gets more confidence the images just get better. Yes, she is an amateur but her attention to detail and willingness to try things and take feedback has made for some really amazing shoots.
