On what was possibly the hottest day of the year, we shot the wedding of Raewyn and Shane. A genuinely lovely couple who chose to get married really close to home on the Te Atatu Peninsula. Surrounded by family and friends at a local waterside park. The styling was very minimal and classy. The ceremony was followed by 90 minutes at the nearby beach shooting the couple in one of their very special places. Places that we hold dear to our hearts are often a wonderful space to get great images. The sun makes for challenging lighting for us photographers but when you have a couple in that “happy place” after sharing their love so publicly you really can’t go wrong.
