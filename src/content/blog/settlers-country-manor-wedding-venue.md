### Intro

Nestled in Kumeu, [**Settlers Country Manor**](https://www.settlerscountrymanor.co.nz/) is one of Auckland’s most versatile wedding venues. With stone buildings, mature gardens, and a riverside lawn, it offers a mix of rustic charm and elegant backdrops in one location. I’ve photographed here in all kinds of light and weather, and it’s a dream venue for both golden-hour portraits and wet-weather indoor shots.

If you’re planning your day here, you’ll want a photographer who knows the **best photo spots, how to structure timelines around the light, and how to keep things stress-free if the weather shifts**. That’s exactly what I bring to the table.

## Best Photo Locations at Settlers Country Manor

One of the strengths of this venue is the variety in such a compact space. Popular backdrops include:

- **The Gardens** – tall trees and manicured paths are perfect for a quiet first look or soft, romantic couple portraits.
- **Stone Buildings** – these textured walls add depth and a timeless, rustic feel, ideal for bridal party group shots.
- **Riverside Lawn** – at sunset, the light here is magical. I always make time for golden-hour portraits by the water.
- **Indoor Spaces** – even if the rain sets in, the verandas and interiors give us warm, atmospheric options.

Because all these locations are within a short walk, we can capture a wide variety of looks without pulling you away from your guests for too long.

## Sample Timelines for Golden-Hour Portraits

Settlers Country Manor is especially beautiful at dusk, so I like to build timelines that make the most of that light. With the **Deluxe Experience** (8+ hours, $3,995), a typical day looks like this:

- **1:30–3:00pm:** Getting ready coverage (bridal party prep + details).
- **3:00–3:30pm:** Ceremony in the gardens or by the riverside.
- **3:30–4:00pm:** Family formals near the stone archway (easy access for guests).
- **4:00–4:45pm:** Bridal party portraits in the gardens.
- **4:45–5:30pm:** Couple portraits by the stone walls and riverside (with flexibility for golden-hour).
- **5:30pm onward:** Reception coverage through to speeches and first dance.

If your ceremony timing shifts, I’ll help you re-balance the schedule so we never miss the best natural light.
