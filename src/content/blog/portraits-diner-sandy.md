Sandra has her own American Diner! I stayed over with her and her hubby on the way to do a shoot (see pics [here](https://www.momentsinlight.co.nz/blog/portraits-sandy)). The diner was there, and it would be mad not to grab some shots right! This place is amazing, with a full kitchen and juke box to coke vending machine, it’s got it all. As usual I roped hubby into holding reflectors and generally helping then as we finished up he even cooked pizza - win!
