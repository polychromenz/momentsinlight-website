Lisa and Anthony brought it in style. This amazing couple had bags of energy and the day was full of lightness, laughs and warm moments.

‘This was such an amazing wedding to photograph for Lisa and Anthony. They live in the same suburb as me, Titirangi, and the whole wedding took place within the Titirangi area.

They had their reception at [French Bay Yacht Club](https://www.frenchbay.org.nz/), which is, my favorite place in the world to photograph. The ceremony was at [Lopdell House](https://lopdellprecinct.org.nz/), on the roof. It’s a spectacular venue to photograph from. We then went to Titirangi Beach to photograph the bride and groom shots, and then on to French Bay Yacht Club.

This wedding was all about interaction with family. They are a beautiful blended family, and to show and capture all those sort of intimate moments and relationships between family members was really important for Lisa and Anthony

I believe this really shows in the photos that I captured.’

David
