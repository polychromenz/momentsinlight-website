The cake rarely gets as much planning attention as the dress or the venue, but it earns its place in the photographs all the same — the design itself, the detail shots before the reception, and of course the cutting, one of the moments every wedding day comes back to. Getting the right baker matters more than it might seem.

‍ ‍

I don't make cakes, but here's a short guide to bakers serving Auckland weddings, to give you a starting point.

‍ ‍

- [**Sweet Bites**](https://sweetbitescakes.co.nz/services/wedding-cakes/) — Personalised, bespoke cakes with a design and tasting consultation included. They run a stress-free process, though availability is limited and bookings go on a first-come basis.
- [**J M Cakery**](https://jmcakery.co.nz/weddings) — Handmade, small-batch cakes using quality ingredients, with a choice of buttercream, fondant, or ganache finishes. The focus is on cakes that look as good as they taste.
- [**K Cake**](https://www.kcake.co.nz/pages/wedding-cakes) — A bespoke design service covering everything from floral and classic to more unconventional styles, plus wedding favours like macarons, cakesicles, and cupcakes if you want to extend the theme beyond the cake table.
- [**Eves Pantry**](https://www.evespantry.co.nz/our-cakes/wedding-cakes/) — Custom designs built around your theme, colours, and patterns, ranging from classical to delicate in style, with plenty of room for your own creative vision.
- [**Rose Crafted Cakes**](https://rccakes.co.nz/) — A premium bespoke baker based in Matakana, serving weddings alongside birthdays and corporate events, with good accommodation for dietary requirements including gluten-free and dairy-free.

‍ ‍
