## Best Photo Locations at Markovina Vineyard Estate

- **The Vines & Gardens** – sweeping views and lush greenery for timeless couple portraits.
- **The Barrel Room & Indoor Spaces** – a dramatic, rustic option that works beautifully if the weather shifts.
- **The Tree-Lined Driveway** – ideal for fun bridal party shots and evening candids.
- **The Lawn & Ceremony Area** – versatile layout for ceremonies; stunning at sunset.
- **The Water Features** – reflective ponds and fountains create dreamy portrait opportunities.

## Wet-Weather Options at Markovina

One of the venue’s biggest strengths is its wet-weather plan. If the rain sets in, we move to the **Barrel Room or covered spaces** for portraits, and I bring **professional off-camera flash** to keep images clean and consistent. The indoor areas have enough space for family groups, while the stone and timber details add atmosphere to portraits.
