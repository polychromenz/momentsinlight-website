Writing your own wedding vows is a beautiful and personal way to express your love and commitment to your partner. Whether you’re planning a New Zealand wedding or any other celebration, heartfelt vows can make your ceremony even more special. Here’s a guide to help you create meaningful and memorable wedding vows:

## 1. Wedding Vow Template

While traditional wedding vows follow a structured format, writing your own allows for more personalisation. Here’s a simple outline to get you started:

1. **Express Your Love**: Begin by saying “I love you.” It may seem obvious, but those three little words hold immense significance.
2. **Promise to Be There**: Acknowledge that you’ll be there through thick and thin. Mention your commitment to supporting each other during both good times and challenging moments.
3. **Share Personal Stories**: Make your vows interesting by sharing personal anecdotes. Talk about your quirks, highs, and lows. Authenticity resonates with your guests and your partner.
4. **Make Actual Promises**: Remember that vows are promises. They’re not just cute anecdotes; they’re a serious commitment. You can promise to stick by your partner’s side forever and even throw in some lighthearted promises (like being the designated spider-killer!).
5. **Acknowledge Support**: Recognise the role of family, friends, and any spiritual beliefs in your marriage. Your loved ones are there to celebrate with you, but they’ll also be there to support you throughout your journey together.

## 2. Wedding Vow Tips

Here are 13 tips to guide you as you write your own wedding vows:

1. **Start with a Description**: Begin by describing who your partner is to you—your best friend, lover, confidant, and more.
2. **Highlight What You Love**: Share what you love about your partner. What qualities make them special? What do you miss most when they’re not around?
3. **Reflect on Your Journey**: Think about your journey together—the moments that brought you closer, the challenges you’ve overcome, and the joy you’ve shared.
4. **Use Specific Details**: Be specific. Instead of saying, “You make me happy,” say, “Your laughter brightens my darkest days.”
5. **Speak from the Heart**: Avoid clichés and generic phrases. Speak genuinely from your heart.
6. **Consider Your Relationship’s Unique Aspects**: What makes your relationship unique? Incorporate those aspects into your vows.
7. **Practice Out Loud**: Read your vows aloud to ensure they flow well and sound natural.
8. **Balance Emotion and Humor**: Mix heartfelt sentiments with light-hearted moments. Laughter is welcome!
9. **Keep It Concise**: Aim for a few minutes of speaking time. Less is often more.
10. **Write Separately**: Consider writing your vows separately to maintain an element of surprise during the ceremony.
11. **Seek Inspiration**: Read poetry, love letters, or watch romantic movies for inspiration.
12. **Edit and Refine**: Revise your vows until they feel just right.
13. **Remember Your Audience**: While personal, your vows are also for your guests. Keep them engaged and touched by your words.

Remember, your wedding vows are a declaration of your love and commitment. Take your time, be sincere, and let your heart guide you. Whether you’re standing on a New Zealand beach or in a cozy chapel, your vows will create a lasting memory for both you and your partner.

*Note: The information provided here is based on general advice and inspiration. Feel free to adapt it to your unique relationship and New Zealand wedding setting.*
