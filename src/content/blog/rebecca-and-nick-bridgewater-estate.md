When couples fully embrace the wedding preparation process, it always shines through in the final images. Rebecca and Nick did exactly that. From our practice shoot to the final timeline details, they brought such an enthusiastic and lovely energy. On their wedding day, all that preparation truly paid off.

The morning began early with bridal prep in the dedicated suite at [Bridgewater Estate](https://www.bridgewaterestate.co.nz/). Venues like Bridgewater are an absolute dream for photography—having beautifully staged, purpose-built spaces gives us so many gorgeous options to work with right from the start.

The weather certainly kept us on our toes with a rainy forecast, but we managed to squeak through and hold the ceremony outside! After all, those stunning grounds are exactly why you choose a venue like Bridgewater. Yes, there were a few umbrellas popping up as a light drizzle fell, but it honestly just added to the romantic, unique experience of the day.

Rebecca and Nick put an incredible amount of effort into their planning, ensuring every detail looked absolutely perfect. They were surrounded by a wonderful supporting cast of friends and family who made the atmosphere incredibly warm and welcoming. While the wet weather meant we couldn't reach some of our planned portrait locations, a big part of the value of having an experienced photographer is being able to flex and adapt. We pivoted, embraced the elements, and still captured some beautiful, intimate moments.

By the end of the night, I found myself in the incredibly fortunate position of sitting down with the newlyweds and their closest family. Sharing quiet conversations and a cup of coffee before heading home—tired, but deeply satisfied—was the perfect way to cap off a truly special day.

**Rebecca said** “*Moments in light is a fantastic photographer to work with. Tony was receptive to all feedback from our practice shoot and brought that into our wedding day. Despite the rain he got some fantastic photos that I will absolutely treasure forever. Because of the rain we got extra shots we didn't originally have time for. Tony is amazing to work with and absolutely worth it! He captured the raw and candid essence of our special day and delivered some incredible photos that captured our love and celebration. He doesn't have a photo or time limit like some other photographers and that meant he was there for us the whole day and we got all of the beautiful photos he took for us. I barely noticed him during the more intimate moments but seeing the photos afterwards he absolutely captured the raw emotions in those moments.*” ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
