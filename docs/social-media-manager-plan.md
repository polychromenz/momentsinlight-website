# Social media manager: plan for Tony to approve

Started 19 Oct 2026. Lives in the studio under a new top menu item, **Social**.

## What Tony asked for

- Instagram, Facebook page, Pinterest and Google Business Profile.
- Drafts appear on their own; **every post is a draft Tony edits and approves** (he often personalises them). Nothing posts without his approval.
- Real weddings, not only the photos in blog posts.
- Couples whose contract says no marketing are never used (the client page's "Their photos can be used in ads and marketing" tick).
- Plan and schedule posts, reuse blog posts, ideas and reminders, stats.

## Rhythm (Tony's choice)

| Platform | Drafts a week | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 3 | 4:5 portrait or a carousel of up to 10 |
| Facebook page | 3 (same days as Instagram, its own caption) | 1–10 photos, link allowed |
| Google Business Profile | 1 | one photo, short update, "Book" or "Learn more" button |
| Pinterest | 5 | 2:3 pins, each linking to a blog post or page |

Drafts are made a week ahead, so there's always a week of posts waiting to be checked.

## Where posts come from

1. **Gallery photos marked "Social"**: the same as the Ad mark. On a gallery's Photos tab, select photos (or a whole section) and mark them **Social**. Couples who said no to marketing are greyed out. Marked photos are spread out so the same wedding doesn't appear twice in a week.
2. **Blog posts**: each wedding blog post becomes an Instagram carousel, a Facebook post with the link, several Pinterest pins and a Google update. Older posts are reused over time.
3. **Google reviews**: a review quote paired with a photo from that couple's wedding (when they're OK for marketing).
4. **Tips and reminders**: planning tips from your guides and FAQs, "2027 dates are filling", seasonal prompts (spring weddings, winter weddings…), and reminders such as "Josh and Paul's gallery is out: post a sneak peek".

## Captions

Drafted by AI in your voice: NZ spelling, warm and relaxed, couples' first names only, venue tagged, a few relevant hashtags (Instagram) or keywords (Pinterest), and no prices unless you add them. Built from the wedding's details, your blog post and the review. You edit every caption before approving.

## The Social page

- **This week and next**: a calendar of drafts by platform and day.
- **Each draft**: the photos (crop chosen per platform, which you can nudge as with Ad picks), the caption to edit, the posting date and time, and **Approve**, **Skip** or **Swap photo**.
- **After approving**:
  - *Now*: **Copy caption** and **Download photos**, so you can post from your phone or computer.
  - *Later*, once a platform is connected: approved posts publish themselves at their scheduled time. You still approve every one.
- **Ideas and reminders**: a short list of suggested posts (e.g. a new review arrived, a gallery was delivered, a blog post went live).
- **Stats** (once connected): reach, likes, saves and clicks per post, and which kinds of posts work best.

## Milestones

1. **Drafts and approval** (first build): Social marks on gallery photos, the weekly drafting from all four sources, AI captions, the Social page with approve, edit, copy and download. No platform connections needed.
2. **Facebook and Instagram connection** (one Meta setup, for your own page and account): approved posts publish themselves, plus their stats.
3. **Google Business Profile**: posts and stats (uses your existing Google connection; Google has to switch on API access for your profile first).
4. **Pinterest**: pins and stats (Pinterest approves API access first).
5. **Stats page**: what's working, across platforms.

Milestones 2 to 4 need you to click through each platform's set-up; I'll guide you through each when we get there.
