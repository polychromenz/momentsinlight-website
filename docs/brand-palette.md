# Moments in Light – Brand palette

The colours for the website refresh (October 2026). The four brand colours from
*Moments in Light-Brand Guidelines.pdf* stay the foundation; the additions extend them for a
warmer, more romantic look. A visual version with swatches is in `brand-palette.html`.

## Brand colours (from the brand guide)

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Peristerá White | `#FFFFFF` | Main page background |
| Griseo Elegance | `#E9ECE6` | Footer and soft bands |
| Timeless Black | `#000000` | Text (body text is softened to `#1A1A1A` on screen) |
| Harmony Green | `#5A5E3B` | Accent: buttons, links, labels, dark green bands |

## Additions

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Champagne | `#F6F1EA` | Warm page background in place of some white; the menu bar. This is the “Champagne White” the brand guide mentions |
| Linen Blush | `#EFE4DC` | A soft, romantic band, used rarely |
| Sage | `#A3B07A` | Fine lines, small icons and the heart. Already on the site as the heart colour. Not for text |
| Deep Olive | `#3E4229` | Darker partner to Harmony Green for full-width bands and the Deluxe package card |
| Antique Gold | `#B39A6B` | Review stars and award marks only. Not for text |

### Helper tints (used on dark green backgrounds)

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pale Sage | `#C9D1A6` | Small labels on Harmony Green or Deep Olive |
| Soft Cream | `#E8E4D6` | Body text on Harmony Green or Deep Olive |
| Light Gold | `#D8C79F` | Review stars on dark green |

## How to combine them

- **Most of the page is light:** white and Champagne, with black text and Harmony Green accents.
- **Two or three dark bands per page** in Harmony Green or Deep Olive (for example a review, the
  closing “check your date” section), with Champagne or Soft Cream text.
- **Buttons:** Harmony Green with white text on light backgrounds; Champagne with Deep Olive
  text on dark backgrounds. Both keep the leaf corner (rounded top-left and bottom-right).
- **Sage and gold are accents only**, never for text: they're too light to read comfortably on
  white.

## Type

Raleway throughout, as the brand guide asks: Light (300) for large headings, Regular (400) for
reading, Medium (500) in wide-spaced capitals for small labels.

## Signature shape

The leaf corner from the site's buttons (rounded top-left and bottom-right, e.g.
`border-radius: 16px 0` on buttons, `32px 0`–`48px 0` on cards and photos).
