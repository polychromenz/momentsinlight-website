# Portrait exports

Every portrait photo on the site that isn't on Cloudflare Images yet: about 389 photos, from the
portrait blog posts and the Portraits, Portraits gallery, Premium Portraits and Sunflowers 2024
pages. (Headshots and Lifestyle came from the current site instead.)

**Export to** `E:\EXPORTS\website\portraits\colour` and `...\portraits\mono`, at 2048px long edge.
Anything Lightroom can't find, I'll download from the current site afterwards, so don't
spend long hunting.

- Most names are camera file names (e.g. `DSC6894`) and also match edits and virtual copies.
  Where several versions show, export the delivered ones (your green or purple label).
- Photos you renamed in Lightroom are searched by their name instead (e.g.
  `Anita_PonsonbyJuly2019_Tonez_0070`, `Courtney00962`). A few can only be searched by a
  date like `20201010`, which shows the whole shoot; export everything it shows and I'll
  pick out the ones the site uses.
