How to combine them

  • Most of each page is light: white and Champagne, black text, Harmony Green accents.
  • Two or three dark bands per page in Harmony Green or Deep Olive.
  • Sage and gold are accents only, never for text.
  • Raleway throughout: Light for big headings, Regular for reading, Medium capitals for labels.
  • The leaf corner (rounded top-left and bottom-right) on buttons, cards and photos.
