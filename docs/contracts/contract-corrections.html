Contract corrections to check

Every change to your contracts, with the sentence before (red) and after (green), and why. Only errors are fixed: a wrong clause reference, grammar, spelling and consistent wording. Nothing changes what the contracts mean. One change is marked please check because it clarifies wording. Nothing is used until you approve it.
