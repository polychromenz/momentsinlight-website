Hey Emma & Liam,

Thank you so much for trusting me to be a part of your special day! Here's some basic info for you moving forward.

Before your wedding

Leading up to your wedding, if you have any questions or need help with anything, please get in touch any time. I'll send you an email about 6 weeks before your wedding to organise a time to catch up for our pre-wedding meeting and practice shoot. That's when we'll go through the schedule of your day in detail and put together an accurate run sheet!

You can always view all of your booking details in your client portal. This is also where your images will show when they're ready.

On the day

Relax and have fun enjoying one of the most amazing days of your life!

After the wedding

I aim to have all your photos retouched and uploaded to your private online gallery within 4 weeks after your wedding. As soon as they're ready, I'll be in touch with a link to all your amazing photos!

As always, feel free to get in touch any time if you have any questions before your big day!
